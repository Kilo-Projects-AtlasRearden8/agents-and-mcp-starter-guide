# What AI Agents Do

You have probably heard people talk about "AI agents." It sounds like something from a spy movie, but the idea is actually simple.

## An agent is an AI with a job and the tools to do it

Think of a regular AI chatbot as a very smart assistant who can talk, write, and answer questions — but who cannot actually *do* anything for you. They can draft an email, but they cannot send it. They can summarize a document, but they cannot move it into a folder.

An **AI agent** is that same smart assistant, plus the ability to take action. An agent can:

- Read files on your computer
- Write or save files
- Send messages or emails (if you give it permission)
- Look up information online
- Chain several steps together to finish a whole task

## A real-world analogy

Imagine hiring an assistant.

- A **plain AI chatbot** is like an assistant who sits in another room. You can ask them anything, and they will give you a great answer — but they cannot leave the room to mail a letter or file a paper.
- An **AI agent** is an assistant who has keys to the office. They can still answer your questions, and now they can also file paperwork, send emails, and organize your desk.

## What agents are good for beginners

You do not need to build an agent from scratch to benefit from one. Today, many AI tools already act like agents for everyday tasks:

- Summarizing long documents and saving the summary as a file
- Organizing downloads into folders by date or type
- Drafting replies to emails and sending them (only when you approve)
- Keeping a running list of to-dos and checking things off

## What agents are NOT

An agent is not magic, and it is not a person. It does not "understand" your life the way you do. It follows patterns it learned during training, and it can make mistakes. That is why every good agent setup includes:

- **Human approval before important actions** (sending email, deleting files, spending money)
- **Clear limits on what the agent can touch** (see the safety rules in this manual)
- **A person in charge** — you

## Quick checklist: spotting an agent

- [ ] Can it take action, or only talk?
- [ ] What files or tools can it reach?
- [ ] Do I have to approve it before it does something important?
- [ ] Who made it, and do I trust them?

## Glossary

- **AI agent**: An AI that can take actions, not just answer questions.
- **Tool**: Something an agent can use, like reading a file or searching the web.
- **Permission**: Your approval for the agent to do something.
